          Calendar alarms instead (rings through your phone's Calendar app)
          
            
            
            
            
            
          
            
          
              
          
            1. Tap Alarm off on any routine item below to switch its alarm on, then choose when to be alerted.
              2. 
          
            2. In your iPhone's Calendar app, create a calendar called Daybook (Calendars → Add Calendar). This keeps your alarms in one place.
              3. 
          
            3. Open Daybook on your iPhone and tap Sync to calendar. Choose Save to Files, open daybook-alarms.ics in Files, tap Add All and pick the Daybook calendar.
              4. 
          
            4. After you change alarms, clear the old ones first so you don't get doubles: Calendar app → Calendars → ⓘ next to Daybook → Delete Calendar. Create it again, then sync.
              5. 
          
            5. Alerts use Calendar's notification sound. Check Settings → Notifications → Calendar if they're silent.
